What it is made of:

- It links the Omniphony rendering engine and its companion crates
  (https://github.com/mgth/Omniphony: orender_engine, renderer, bridge_api,
  runtime_control, script_backend and others), which are GPL-3.0-or-later, so
  the library is a combined work under the GPL.
- The Dolby and DTS decoders of harletty-bridge are linked in (they are the
  same code as the harletty_dolby_bridge and harletty_dts_bridge plugins).
  Most of the harletty-bridge crates are Apache-2.0 (LICENSE.Apache-2.0).
- The DTS decoder statically links the `dca` crate, which is LGPL-2.1-or-later
  (LICENSE.LGPL-2.1) because it is based on FFmpeg's DCA decoder; its
  copyright holders are listed in NOTICE.dca.
- The engine's scripting support links `mlua` with a bundled Lua (MIT).
- Windows API bindings come from the `windows` crates (MIT OR Apache-2.0).
- Other third-party Rust crates are under permissive licences (MIT,
  Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unlicense, 0BSD). The one exception is
  `generational-arena` (MPL-2.0), used unmodified; its source is at
  https://crates.io/crates/generational-arena.

Apache-2.0, LGPL-2.1-or-later, MPL-2.0 and the permissive licences above may
all be combined into a work under GPL-3.0. Linking this library into a
proprietary program is not permitted.

The complete corresponding source is the harletty-bridge repository at the
tag of this release (https://github.com/harletty/harletty-bridge), together
with the Omniphony commit named in its `.omniphony-ref` file.
